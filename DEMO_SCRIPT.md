# ProofPay: 3-minute demo script

**Before recording**
- Open https://proofpay-acp.onrender.com a minute early so the free instance is awake.
- Agent network tab: press **Reset all track records** at the bottom, so the run follows the story below.
- Log in to the PayPal sandbox buyer once in the same browser, so the approval window goes straight to **Pay**.
- Browser at 1280 to 1440 px wide, zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden. No background music.
- Keep a second tab on the PayPal sandbox dashboard (developer.paypal.com, Event Logs or the business sandbox account's activity) for the closing shot.

Total spoken text is about 400 words, which fits 2:50 at a calm pace.

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | The header: "Your agents buy. PayPal pays only when the delivery checks out." | "I run a small online shop that sells paper business forms. I source from overseas suppliers, and the rule is pay first and hope. If the carton arrives short, or in a cheaper paper, my money is already gone." |
| 0:15 | Point at the request sentence and the limits line. | "ProofPay lets AI agents buy for me, and PayPal only pays once the delivery is verified. I write the order as one sentence, with a price limit. The spend limit and the approval are mine; the agents can't change either." |
| 0:30 | Press **Send agents**. Step 2 lights up, offer cards fill in. | "My buyer agent invites five supplier agents. Four are the most trusted for this product. The fifth, QuickForm, isn't trusted enough, so it paid twenty-five dollars of its own earnings for a sponsored slot. Every quote and counter-offer here is written by Claude." |
| 0:55 | The navy banner: "Approve a hold of $… for QuickForm Trading." Press **Approve hold in PayPal**, then **Pay** in the PayPal window. | "QuickForm is cheapest, so it goes first. Before anything moves, I approve a hold in PayPal. This is an authorization, not a payment." |
| 1:15 | "Where your money is": the amount slides into the blue Held by PayPal column. | "The money is held by PayPal. Now the supplier delivers." |
| 1:25 | Delivery check: two coral Mismatch rows, and the "paypal hold" row. The amount slides back to Your PayPal. | "An independent evaluator agent first confirms on PayPal, using the PayPal Agent Toolkit, that my money really is held. Then it checks the delivery: 460 books instead of 500, and two-part paper instead of three. It rejects the job, the authorization is voided, and I was never charged." |
| 1:50 | Second banner for Hanbit; approve and press **Pay**. The amount slides to Supplier and turns neon green. Step 5: Supplier paid. | "My agent stops chasing price and takes the most trusted supplier left. This delivery matches everything, so PayPal captures the payment and the supplier is paid." |
| 2:15 | Switch to **Agent network**: the paid tally, QuickForm's trust dropping and its row falling, the earned column. | "Every agent works for its own account. Suppliers earn the sale, evaluators earn a fee for each check whatever the verdict, and my agent keeps a share of what it saved me. QuickForm's trust score just fell, and its advertising bought it a chance, not my money." |
| 2:35 | Scroll to a supplier's italic "Its own call" line, then the PayPal sandbox dashboard showing the voided authorization and the capture. | "After every order each supplier decides on its own how to price its next bid and whether to advertise. And here are the same two transactions in the PayPal sandbox: one voided, one captured." |
| 2:50 | Back to the header. | "No crypto wallet, no smart contract. Just PayPal authorize, capture and void, with agents that have to earn their payout. That's ProofPay." |
